Privacy policy for the Jarvis text notification program

Effective 28 September 2026. Program overview · Terms

What is collected

When you enroll, the operator (David Mendlen, sole proprietor) records your name as given, your mobile number, and the date and time you typed I AGREE. When you reply to a message, the text of your reply is received so that Jarvis can act on it (for example, locking a door you asked it to lock).

How it is used

Your mobile number is used for one purpose: to send you the household notifications described in the program overview and to receive your replies. The consent record is kept to show that you agreed to receive them.

Sharing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your number is passed only to the messaging carrier (Twilio) as is necessary to deliver the messages you consented to.

Retention

Your number and consent record are kept while you are enrolled. When you reply STOP or ask to be removed, your number is removed from the list of recipients; the dated record of your consent and of your opt-out is kept as evidence that both happened.

Security

Records are stored on the household's own server, which is not reachable from the public internet.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP, or email jarvisdave2001@gmail.com, to ask what is held about your number or to have it deleted.

Changes

Changes to this policy are made by updating this page and its effective date.
