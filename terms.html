You can enroll only in person, on the household's server, by reading the program notice, entering your own mobile number and typing the words I AGREE. Consent is recorded with a timestamp. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase; nothing is for sale.

Message frequency varies, typically 1–5 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan. The operator does not charge for these messages.

4. Cancelling

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. CANCEL, END, QUIT and UNSUBSCRIBE are also honoured. You may also ask the operator to remove your number.
